## Why the AGPL

PyMuPDF can only be used for free under the AGPL-3.0. Whoever offers GroundTwin to others over a
network must offer them the source code of the version they run (AGPL § 13). The app does this with a
*Quellcode* link on the start page and in the menu; it points to this repository
(`GEO_AGENT_QUELLCODE` changes it for a fork). Details and alternatives (commercial PyMuPDF license,
switching to pypdfium2): [docs/14](docs/14_licensing.md).

## Data

- The examples in `data/beispiele` are factual (coordinates, Schichten) or synthetic data; third-party
  PDFs are **not** in the repository (only URL and `sha256`).
- `services/geodaten/geodaten/boreholeml_codes.json` holds codes and names taken from the BoreholeML
  codelists v2 of the AK BoreholeML4 of the state geological surveys
  ([gitlab.opencode.de/ak-boreholeml4/boreholeml](https://gitlab.opencode.de/ak-boreholeml4/boreholeml)),
  MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023 Bundesanstalt für Geowissenschaften und Rohstoffe. It is generated by
  `services/geodaten/werkzeuge/boreholeml_codes.py`, which records the version of every list.
- The LoD2 buildings of the Länder are fetched at runtime by the user (nothing is shipped). Each building in
  the IFC carries the license and attribution of its service (`CPset_Gebaeude`): Berlin dl-de/zero-2.0,
  Brandenburg „© GeoBasis-DE/LGB, dl-de/by-2-0“, NRW „Geobasis NRW, dl-de/zero-2-0“.
- The DGM of the Länder is fetched at runtime by the user (nothing is shipped). Its attribution goes
  with the DGM into the IFC (`CPset_Gelaende`): Brandenburg with Berlin „© GeoBasis-DE/LGB, dl-de/by-2-0;
  © Geoportal Berlin, dl-de/by-2-0“, NRW „Geobasis NRW, dl-de/zero-2-0“.
- Bohrungen der Länder are fetched at runtime by the user (nothing is shipped). The app shows the
  attribution of every service (`services/geodaten/geodaten/dienste.json`): Berlin dl-de/zero-2.0,
  Brandenburg and NRW dl-de/by-2.0. Baden-Württemberg names no license in its service; check it
  before passing data on. An imported Bohrung keeps its source, license and date in the IFC
  (`CPset_Herkunft`). The screenshot `docs/img/21_standort_vorerkundung.jpg` shows a model from
  Bohrungen NRW: „© Geowissenschaftliche Daten: Bohrungen NRW @ Geologischer Dienst NRW, 2026“,
  dl-de/by-2.0.
- The quasigeoid **GCG2016** of the Bundesamt für Kartographie und Geodäsie, as the PROJ-data file
  `de_bkg_gcg2016.tif` (CC BY 4.0, „© Bundesamt für Kartographie und Geodäsie (BKG), GCG2016“), is fetched at
  runtime on the first 3D Tiles export, checked by its sha256 and kept in the work folder; nothing is shipped.
  The attribution goes into every tileset (`asset.extras.Namensnennung`) and its `LIESMICH.txt`, with those of
  the DGM, the buildings and the Bohrungen in the model. The screenshot `docs/img/21_cesium_3dtiles.jpg` shows
  the Berlin example with the DGM of Brandenburg with Berlin („© GeoBasis-DE/LGB, dl-de/by-2-0; © Geoportal
  Berlin, dl-de/by-2-0“), the LoD2 buildings of Berlin (dl-de/zero-2.0) and heights with the GCG2016 (© BKG,
  CC BY 4.0).
- The map of the Standort card loads its tiles at runtime in the user's browser (nothing is shipped):
  basemap.de Web Raster of the BKG, CC BY 4.0, „© GeoBasis-DE / BKG (year) CC BY 4.0“, shown in the map; or
  OpenStreetMap tiles under the [tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/),
  „© OpenStreetMap-Mitwirkende“ (ODbL). The screenshot `docs/img/21_standort_karte.jpg` shows the basemap.de map
  („© GeoBasis-DE / BKG (2026) CC BY 4.0“).
- `services/geodaten/geodaten/geoldg.json` paraphrases the Geologiedatengesetz with § references and links to
  gesetze-im-internet.de; laws are official works without copyright (§ 5 Abs. 1 UrhG).
- The norms database (`services/modeler/baugrundmodell/normen.json`) does **not** reproduce texts of
  DIN 4023, DIN EN ISO 14688-1 or DIN 18196: it collects abbreviations and colours with the evidence
  for every value.
